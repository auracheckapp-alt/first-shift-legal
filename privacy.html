Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

The short version: there's no account, and your resumes are saved only on your phone. When you use an AI feature, your resume text is sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude AI to make your results. Our server doesn't store it, and it isn't used to train AI. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.

Who we are

First Shift ("we", "us") is a resume app for people looking for entry-level jobs, operated by Austin Nieves. Contact us at auracheck.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is never uploaded to us for storage:

  • Your saved resumes (the text you paste, or the text read from a PDF or Word file you upload) and the names you give them.
  • Tailored versions of your resumes and any edits you make to them.
  • Your results: resume scores, job matches and job fit checks.
  • Your choice about AI processing, and whether you've finished the intro screens.

When you upload a file, the app reads its text on your phone and deletes its temporary copy right away. The file itself is never uploaded.

No accounts

You don't sign up or log in. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your resume may contain that kind of information; it stays on your phone except as described in the next section.

When you use an AI feature

First Shift asks for your permission before any resume text leaves your phone. After you agree, each time you run Resume Score, Best Job Matches, Job Fit or Tailor, the app sends:

  • the text of the resume you picked,
  • a job posting you paste or a job title you type, if the feature uses one,
  • your anonymous device ID (see below),

over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server, which runs on Cloudflare. Our server passes the text to Anthropic's Claude AI, checks the answer, and sends the result back to your phone. Our server does not store your resume or job posting text, and does not write it to logs. It is held in memory only while your request is being handled.

Anthropic processes the text as our service provider to generate your result. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data is not used to train its AI models. Anthropic may keep it for a limited time to keep its service safe and prevent misuse, as described in Anthropic's privacy policy.

You can turn off AI features at any time in Settings → AI & privacy. After that, nothing more is sent until you agree again.

Anonymous device ID and usage limits

On first launch, the app creates a random ID and keeps it in your iPhone's Keychain, so it stays the same if you reinstall the app. It isn't linked to your name, Apple ID or any other identifier. The app sends it with each AI request, and our server stores only that ID, which feature was used, and when. We use this to apply free-use limits and a daily fair-use limit. We don't use it to track you or for advertising.

Like any website or server, our host Cloudflare processes basic technical information such as your IP address to deliver requests, and keeps short request logs (for example times and error codes) to keep the service running. These logs never contain your resume or job posting text.

Purchases

The one-time Lifetime Unlock is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage it. RevenueCat receives your anonymous device ID and your purchase status from the App Store, along with basic technical information (such as device model, iOS version, app version, country and IP address), so it can confirm the purchase. Our server asks RevenueCat whether your device ID has unlocked, to apply the right limits. RevenueCat never receives your resumes. We never receive your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing and PDF export

When you export a PDF or share your score or top jobs, the file or image is made on your phone and Apple's share sheet opens. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose where to send it. Share images never include your name or resume text.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We don't store your resumes or job postings on our server.
  • We don't use your resumes to train AI, and Anthropic doesn't either.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete all my data to permanently delete your resumes, tailored versions, results and settings from your phone. Deleting the app also removes them. Your anonymous usage records (the device ID, feature and time) are kept to apply usage limits; to ask us to delete them, email auracheck.app@gmail.com with the approximate time you used the app. To ask RevenueCat to delete the anonymous purchase record, email us too.

Children

First Shift is meant for people 13 and older who are looking for work. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Your saved data is protected by your device's security. Everything the app sends to our server, Anthropic and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because your resumes stay on your device, you can view, edit and delete them directly in the app. For anything else, contact auracheck.app@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
